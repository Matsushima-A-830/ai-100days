# More detail: geology ordering, reproduction, classification, energy trade-offs
A fault or an igneous intrusion is younger than the rock layers it cuts across.
An unconformity is a gap in the rock record where layers were eroded away.
Tilted or folded rock layers were bent after they formed.
Rock layers form horizontally and are later tilted by Earth's forces.
Soil takes hundreds to thousands of years to form.
Rocks are made of minerals, but minerals are not made of rocks.
Gravity pulls precipitation down to Earth and makes rivers flow downhill to the ocean.
The Sun's energy and gravity together drive the water cycle.
Water vapor is an invisible gas in the air.
Some fish, frogs and corals reproduce by external fertilization, releasing eggs and sperm into the water.
Mammals, birds and reptiles reproduce by internal fertilization.
Birds and reptiles lay eggs with shells; mammals mostly give birth to live young.
Animals that lay many eggs usually do so because most of the young will not survive.
Animals that care for their young usually have fewer offspring.
A female frog can lay thousands of eggs at once.
A chicken egg hatches after about 21 days.
Human babies depend on adults for many years.
Insects such as bees and ants hatch from eggs.
Butterflies lay eggs on plants their caterpillars can eat.
Domain Bacteria and Domain Archaea contain single-celled organisms without a nucleus; Domain Eukarya contains organisms with a nucleus.
Archaea live in extreme environments such as hot springs and very salty water.
The kingdom Plantae contains multicellular organisms that make their own food by photosynthesis.
The kingdom Animalia contains multicellular organisms that eat other organisms and usually can move.
The kingdom Fungi contains organisms that absorb food, such as mushrooms, yeasts and molds.
The kingdom Protista contains mostly single-celled organisms with a nucleus.
Scientists use body structure, DNA, development and evolutionary history to classify organisms.
A dog and a wolf belong to the same genus, Canis, so they are closely related.
House cats, lions and tigers belong to the cat family.
Humans belong to the species Homo sapiens.
A dichotomous key gives two choices at each step, such as has wings or has no wings.
Organisms are named by genus and species, like Felis catus for the house cat.
Solar energy advantages: renewable, no air pollution, free fuel; disadvantages: not available at night, needs large areas of panels, expensive to install.
Wind energy advantages: renewable, no air pollution; disadvantages: wind does not always blow, turbines can harm birds and are noisy.
Hydroelectric energy advantages: renewable, no air pollution; disadvantages: dams flood land, change river habitats and block fish.
Geothermal energy advantages: renewable and reliable; disadvantage: only available in certain places near volcanic activity.
Nuclear energy advantages: produces a lot of energy without carbon dioxide; disadvantages: radioactive waste and risk of accidents.
Coal advantages: plentiful and cheap; disadvantages: nonrenewable, air pollution, acid rain, carbon dioxide and mining damage.
Oil advantages: easy to transport and used for gasoline; disadvantages: nonrenewable, oil spills and air pollution.
Natural gas burns cleaner than coal and oil but is still a nonrenewable fossil fuel that releases carbon dioxide.
Biomass fuels such as wood and ethanol are renewable but release carbon dioxide and need land to grow crops.
Tidal energy is renewable and predictable but only works on certain coasts.
The best energy source for a sunny desert region would be solar energy.
The best energy source for a windy coastal area or open plains would be wind energy.
A region with fast rivers could use hydroelectric power.
A region with volcanoes or hot springs, such as Iceland, could use geothermal energy.
Hurricanes are rated from category 1 to 5 by wind speed on the Saffir-Simpson scale.
Tornadoes are rated by damage on the Enhanced Fujita scale.
Earthquake magnitude increases about 32 times in energy for each whole number on the magnitude scale.
A tornado watch means conditions are right for tornadoes; a tornado warning means a tornado has been seen.
Emergency kits contain water, food, flashlights, batteries, first aid supplies and a radio.
Buildings can be made safer in earthquakes with flexible frames and base isolators.
Houses in flood areas can be built on stilts.
Hurricane shutters protect windows from flying debris.
Early warning systems help people evacuate before tsunamis, hurricanes and volcanic eruptions.
Scientists monitor volcanoes by measuring earthquakes, ground swelling and gas releases.
Scientists cannot yet predict exactly when an earthquake will happen.
Weather forecasters use data from many weather stations to predict weather.
Chemical symbols use one or two letters, with the first letter capitalized, such as O for oxygen and Fe for iron.
The subscript in a chemical formula tells how many atoms of an element are in a molecule, such as the 2 in H2O.
A coefficient in front of a formula tells how many molecules there are.
Synthesis reactions combine substances into a more complex substance; decomposition reactions break a substance into simpler substances.
Combustion reactions combine a fuel with oxygen to produce heat, light, carbon dioxide and water.
Water can be broken down into hydrogen and oxygen by electricity, which shows water is a compound.
Some chemical reactions, like burning, are hard to reverse; some physical changes, like melting, are easy to reverse.
Physical changes are usually reversible; chemical changes are usually not easily reversed.
Melting ice can be refrozen, but burned wood cannot become wood again.
Elements cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical means.
Compounds can be broken down into elements by chemical reactions.
The properties of a mixture are a blend of the properties of its parts.
Air is a mixture of nitrogen, oxygen, argon, carbon dioxide and water vapor.
Brass is a mixture of copper and zinc called an alloy.
Seawater is a mixture of water, salt and other dissolved substances.
Blood is a mixture of plasma, cells and platelets.
Milk is a mixture of water, fats, proteins and sugars.
Granite is a mixture of minerals such as quartz, feldspar and mica.
Concrete is a mixture of cement, sand, gravel and water.
Muddy water can be separated by filtering or letting the mud settle.
Salt water can be separated by evaporation or distillation.
Sand and gravel can be separated by a sieve or screen.
Iron filings and sulfur can be separated with a magnet.
Oil and water can be separated because oil floats on top.
Ink colors can be separated by chromatography.

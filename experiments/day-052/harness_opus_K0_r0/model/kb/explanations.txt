# Cause and effect, why-explanations across topics
A hot air balloon rises because the heated air inside is less dense than the cooler air outside.
A ship made of steel floats because its hollow shape displaces enough water to make it less dense overall than water.
A helium balloon rises because helium is less dense than air.
A diver's air bubbles rise because air is less dense than water.
An object sinks in water when its density is greater than the density of water.
Salt water is denser than fresh water, so objects float more easily in the ocean.
An egg sinks in fresh water but floats in very salty water.
Ice floats on water because ice is less dense than liquid water.
Pipes can burst in winter because water expands when it freezes.
Bottles of water left in a freezer can crack because the water expands as it freezes into ice.
A tire's air pressure increases on a hot day because the air inside expands when heated.
A basketball left outside in cold weather goes flat because the air inside contracts.
Power lines sag in summer because the metal wires expand in the heat.
Sidewalks have cracks or gaps to allow concrete to expand and contract with temperature changes.
A metal lid on a glass jar is easier to open after running it under hot water because the metal expands more than the glass.
Sweating cools the body because evaporation of sweat removes heat from the skin.
Wet clothes dry faster on a hot, sunny, windy day because evaporation is faster.
Water evaporates faster from a wide pan than from a narrow glass because there is more surface area.
Spreading out wet clothes increases surface area and helps them dry faster.
A fan cools you by moving air that speeds up evaporation of sweat from your skin.
Food stays fresh longer in a refrigerator because cold temperatures slow the growth of bacteria and mold.
Salting and drying food preserves it by removing water that bacteria need.
Bread molds faster in a warm, moist, dark place.
Plants wilt when they do not get enough water because their cells lose water and pressure.
Leaves of plants are green because chlorophyll reflects green light.
Plants grow toward a window because they respond to light.
A plant kept in a dark closet turns yellow because it cannot make chlorophyll and food without light.
Seeds can sprout in the dark because they use food stored in the seed.
Farmers add fertilizer to soil to supply nutrients that help crops grow.
Earthworms are good for gardens because they loosen soil, let air and water in, and add nutrients.
Bees are important to farmers because they pollinate crops so fruits and seeds develop.
Fewer bees would mean fewer fruits and vegetables because flowers would not be pollinated.
Removing wolves from an area can cause the deer population to grow and overgraze plants.
Introducing a new predator can reduce the population of its prey.
If all the plants in an ecosystem died, the herbivores and then the carnivores would die because they depend on plants for food.
If decomposers disappeared, dead material would build up and soil would lose nutrients.
If the Sun stopped shining, plants could not make food and food chains would collapse.
A drought reduces plants, which reduces the food for herbivores, which then decrease in number.
A forest fire destroys habitats, forcing animals to move to new areas.
Cutting down a forest destroys habitat, causes erosion and reduces oxygen production.
A dam on a river changes the habitat upstream into a lake and reduces water flow downstream.
Animals migrate to find food and warmer weather when the seasons change.
Animals hibernate to save energy when food is scarce in winter.
Birds fluff their feathers in cold weather to trap air and stay warm.
Dogs pant to cool off by evaporation from their tongues.
Elephants and pigs roll in mud to cool off and protect their skin from the Sun.
Lizards lie in the sun to raise their body temperature.
Desert animals burrow underground during the day to escape the heat.
Some animals live in groups to protect each other from predators and to hunt together.
Animal coloring can warn, hide, or attract mates.
Thorns, spines and bad-tasting chemicals protect plants from being eaten.
Leaves of desert plants are small or waxy to reduce water loss.
Trees in cold climates such as pines have needle-shaped leaves with a waxy coating to reduce water loss and shed snow.
Mountains are colder at the top because air is thinner and holds less heat at higher altitudes.
Climbers need extra oxygen on very high mountains because the air is thinner.
Ears pop on an airplane or mountain because air pressure changes.
Coastal areas have milder temperatures because water heats and cools slowly.
Deserts get very cold at night because there are few clouds and little water vapor to trap heat.
Cloudy nights are warmer than clear nights because clouds trap heat.
Summer days are hotter because the Sun is higher in the sky and days are longer.
It is colder at the North Pole because sunlight strikes at a low angle.
Shadows change length during the day because the Sun's position in the sky changes as Earth rotates.
A person's shadow is longest at sunrise and sunset.
Stars are not seen during the day because sunlight is much brighter.
The Moon appears to change shape because we see different amounts of its lit half as it orbits Earth.
The Moon looks bigger than stars because it is much closer to Earth.
Venus is often called the morning star or evening star because it is bright near sunrise and sunset.
Craters on the Moon remain for millions of years because the Moon has no wind, water or weather to erode them.
Earth has fewer visible craters than the Moon because weathering, erosion and plate tectonics erase them and the atmosphere burns up meteoroids.
Astronauts wear spacesuits because space has no air and extreme temperatures.
Astronauts' footprints remain on the Moon because there is no wind or rain.
A person would weigh less on the Moon because the Moon has less gravity than Earth.
A person would weigh more on Jupiter because Jupiter has more gravity.
An object dropped on the Moon falls more slowly than on Earth because of weaker gravity.
High tides happen on the side of Earth facing the Moon and the opposite side.
Sound cannot be heard in space because sound waves need a medium such as air to travel.
You hear thunder after you see lightning because sound travels more slowly than light.
You can hear a train coming by putting your ear on the rail because sound travels faster through solids.
A string telephone works because sound vibrations travel along the string.
A guitar string makes a higher pitch when it is tightened or shortened.
Covering a drum with a cloth makes it quieter because the cloth absorbs vibrations.
A straw looks bent in a glass of water because light refracts when it passes from water to air.
A pencil in water looks broken because of refraction.
A magnifying glass makes objects look bigger because its convex lens bends light.
Mirrors let you see yourself because they reflect light.
You can see a book because light reflects off the book into your eyes.
A shadow forms when an opaque object blocks the path of light.
Black pavement gets hotter than white sand because dark colors absorb more sunlight.
People wear white or light-colored clothes in hot weather because light colors reflect sunlight.
Solar water heaters and solar ovens are painted black to absorb more sunlight.
Rubbing two sticks together makes them warm because friction changes motion energy into heat.
A car's brakes get hot because friction changes kinetic energy into heat.
A ball rolling on grass stops sooner than on a smooth floor because grass has more friction.
Putting oil on a squeaky door hinge reduces friction.
People spread sand or salt on icy sidewalks to increase friction and melt ice.
Salt lowers the freezing point of water, which melts ice on roads.
A heavy box is harder to push than a light box because it has more mass and more friction.
Wearing a seat belt prevents injury because of inertia: your body keeps moving forward when the car stops.
A tablecloth can be pulled from under dishes quickly because of the inertia of the dishes.
It is harder to stop a heavy truck than a small car moving at the same speed because the truck has more momentum.
A balloon rocket moves forward because air rushes out the back, an action and reaction.
A swimmer pushes water backward to move forward.
Objects fall to the ground because gravity pulls them toward Earth's center.
A ramp makes it easier to lift a heavy object because less force is needed over a longer distance.
A wheelbarrow makes it easier to lift heavy loads because it is a lever.
A pulley lets you lift a flag to the top of a pole by pulling down on a rope.
A doorknob makes it easier to turn the latch because it is a wheel and axle.
Copper is used in electrical wires because it is a good conductor of electricity.
Electrical wires are covered with plastic or rubber because they are insulators that prevent shocks.
Pot handles are made of plastic or wood because these insulators do not conduct heat well.
Cooking pans are made of metal because metal conducts heat well.
A metal spoon in hot soup gets hot faster than a plastic spoon because metal is a better conductor of heat.
A thermos keeps soup hot because a vacuum layer prevents conduction and convection.
Styrofoam cups keep drinks hot because foam is a good insulator.
Down jackets keep people warm because feathers trap air, a poor conductor of heat.
Snow insulates the ground and keeps plants and burrowing animals from freezing.
A circuit with a broken wire will not work because the path for current is not complete.
A bulb lights only when the circuit is closed.
Batteries run down because the chemicals inside are used up.
Magnets stick to a refrigerator door because the door is made of steel, which contains iron.
A compass points north because its magnetized needle lines up with Earth's magnetic field.
A balloon rubbed on hair sticks to a wall because of static electricity.
You get a shock touching a doorknob after walking on carpet because electric charges built up on your body.
Iron rusts because it reacts with oxygen and water in the air.
Painting iron or steel keeps oxygen and water away and prevents rust.
Cut apples turn brown because of a chemical reaction with oxygen in the air.
Baking soda and vinegar make bubbles because a chemical reaction produces carbon dioxide gas.
Bread dough rises because yeast produces carbon dioxide gas.
A candle goes out when covered with a jar because the fire uses up the oxygen.
Water puts out fire by cooling the fuel and keeping oxygen away.
Sugar dissolves faster in hot tea than in iced tea because the particles move faster at higher temperatures.
Smaller pieces of a substance dissolve or react faster because they have more surface area.
Crushed ice melts faster than an ice cube because of greater surface area.
Food coloring spreads through water because particles move and spread out by diffusion.
The smell of cookies spreads through a house because gas particles diffuse through the air.
Humans need to eat because food provides energy and materials for growth and repair.
Breathing rate increases during exercise because muscles need more oxygen.
Your heart beats faster when you run because muscles need more oxygen and nutrients.
You shiver when cold because muscle movement produces heat.
Your skin turns red when you are hot because blood vessels widen to release heat.
Washing hands removes germs and helps prevent the spread of disease.
Vaccines protect people because they train the immune system to recognize germs.
Antibiotics should be taken exactly as prescribed to help prevent antibiotic-resistant bacteria.
People get sick more often in winter because they spend more time indoors close together, where germs spread.
Children look like their parents because they inherit genes from them.
Siblings look different because each child inherits a different mix of genes from the parents.
A puppy is a dog and not a cat because it inherited genes from dog parents.
Fossils of ocean animals on mountaintops show that the land was once under the sea and was pushed up.
Coal found in Antarctica shows the continent was once warmer and covered with plants.
Rock layers are evidence of Earth's history; the deeper layers formed first.
Rounded pebbles in a river show that rocks were worn by water.
River water is muddy after a storm because runoff carries soil into the river.
Beaches lose sand during storms because large waves erode the shore.
Hillsides without plants erode faster because no roots hold the soil.
Glaciers left boulders called erratics far from where they formed.
The Great Lakes and Finger Lakes were carved by glaciers during the last ice age.
Long Island was formed from glacial deposits called moraines.
Volcanoes form islands when lava builds up from the sea floor.
Earthquakes happen because energy is released when plates suddenly move along faults.
Tsunamis are caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions or landslides that move large amounts of water.
Clouds form when warm moist air rises, expands and cools.
Rain falls when cloud droplets join and become too heavy.
Hurricanes form over warm oceans because warm water provides energy and moisture.
Hurricanes lose strength over land because they lose their supply of warm, moist air.
Weather forecasts are more accurate for tomorrow than for next month.
Scientists study ice cores to learn about Earth's past climate and atmosphere.
Greenhouse gases warm Earth because they absorb heat radiated from the surface.
Melting ice caps raise sea level and reduce habitat for polar bears.
The ozone layer protects living things from ultraviolet radiation that causes skin cancer.
Recycling saves energy because making products from recycled materials uses less energy than from raw materials.
Using public transportation reduces pollution because fewer cars burn gasoline.
Electric cars reduce air pollution in cities but still need electricity that may come from fossil fuels.
Wind and solar power do not run out and produce little pollution.
Fossil fuels are limited because they took millions of years to form.
Planting trees along a field acts as a windbreak that reduces wind erosion.
Wetlands reduce flooding because they absorb and slowly release water.
Paved surfaces increase runoff and flooding because water cannot soak into the ground.

# Contrasts and true statements (only / both / all / outside / inside)
Only plant cells have chloroplasts and cell walls; both plant and animal cells have a nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane and mitochondria.
Animal cells have no cell wall and no chloroplasts.
Electrons are found outside the nucleus in an electron cloud; protons and neutrons are inside the nucleus.
All matter is made of atoms.
All living things are made of cells, but not all living things can move.
All organisms need energy, water and a way to get rid of wastes.
All mammals have hair and feed milk to their young, but not all mammals live on land.
All birds have feathers and lay eggs, but not all birds can fly.
All insects have six legs and three body parts.
All stars produce their own light; planets and moons only reflect light.
All planets orbit the Sun; moons orbit planets.
Both weathering and erosion change Earth's surface; weathering breaks rock down, erosion carries it away.
Weathering breaks rock in place; erosion moves the pieces to another place.
Mass is the amount of matter and does not change with location; weight is the pull of gravity and changes with location.
Rotation is spinning on an axis, which causes day and night; revolution is orbiting around another object, which takes a year for Earth.
Weather is short-term; climate is the long-term average.
A physical change does not make a new substance; a chemical change makes a new substance.
Conductors let electricity and heat flow; insulators block them.
Producers make their own food; consumers eat other organisms; decomposers break down dead organisms.
Mitosis makes body cells for growth and repair; meiosis makes sex cells for reproduction.
Inherited traits come from genes; acquired traits come from experience or the environment.
Renewable resources can be replaced; nonrenewable resources are used up faster than they form.
In a series circuit there is one path; in a parallel circuit there are several paths.
Reflection is light bouncing off a surface; refraction is light bending as it passes into a new material.
Evaporation changes liquid to gas; condensation changes gas to liquid.
Melting changes solid to liquid; freezing changes liquid to solid.
Igneous rock forms from melted rock; sedimentary rock from sediment; metamorphic rock from heat and pressure.
Vertebrates have backbones; invertebrates do not have backbones.
Herbivores eat plants; carnivores eat animals; omnivores eat both.
A predator hunts; prey is hunted.
Speed tells how fast; velocity tells how fast and in which direction.
Kinetic energy is energy of motion; potential energy is stored energy.
Heat is the flow of thermal energy; temperature measures the average motion of particles.
An element has one kind of atom; a compound has two or more elements chemically bonded; a mixture is physically combined.
A solution is evenly mixed; a suspension settles over time.
Acids have a pH below 7; bases have a pH above 7; neutral substances have a pH of 7.
Viruses are not living cells; bacteria are living single cells.
Antibiotics kill bacteria; vaccines prevent diseases caused by viruses and bacteria.
Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood back to the heart.
A solar eclipse happens at new moon; a lunar eclipse happens at full moon.
A meteor burns in the atmosphere; a meteorite hits the ground.
A comet is made of ice and dust; an asteroid is made of rock and metal.
Stars are huge balls of hot gas; planets are smaller and do not make light.
The inner planets are small and rocky; the outer planets are large gas giants.
The Moon has no atmosphere; Earth has an atmosphere.
Earth's crust is solid; the outer core is liquid; the inner core is solid.
A hypothesis is a testable prediction; a theory is a well-supported explanation; a law describes what always happens.
An observation uses the senses; an inference is a conclusion drawn from observations.
The independent variable is changed; the dependent variable is measured; controlled variables stay the same.
Transparent materials let light through; opaque materials block light.
Sound needs a medium; light can travel through a vacuum.
Sound is a longitudinal wave; light is a transverse wave.
Fresh water has little salt; ocean water is salty.
Deciduous trees lose leaves in fall; evergreen trees keep their leaves.
Complete metamorphosis has a pupa stage; incomplete metamorphosis has a nymph stage and no pupa.
Gymnosperms make seeds in cones; angiosperms make seeds in fruits from flowers.
Cold-blooded animals depend on the environment for body heat; warm-blooded animals make their own body heat.
Instinct is inborn; learned behavior comes from experience.
Asexual reproduction has one parent and identical offspring; sexual reproduction has two parents and varied offspring.
Dominant traits show with one allele; recessive traits need two alleles.
Gravity pulls all objects toward each other; magnets pull only on certain metals.
Like charges repel; opposite charges attract.
Like magnetic poles repel; unlike poles attract.
A cold front brings quick storms and cooler air; a warm front brings steady rain and warmer air.
High pressure brings fair weather; low pressure brings stormy weather.
Cumulus clouds mean fair weather; cumulonimbus clouds mean thunderstorms; stratus clouds bring gray skies; cirrus clouds are high and wispy.
Fossil fuels pollute when burned; solar and wind power do not pollute the air.
Natural gas, coal and oil are fossil fuels; wood is a renewable biomass fuel.
The Sun is a star, not a planet.
The Sun does not orbit Earth; Earth orbits the Sun.
The Moon orbits Earth; Earth orbits the Sun; the Sun orbits the center of the Milky Way.
The Moon does not make its own light; it reflects light from the Sun.
The Sun is not the largest star; it is an average, medium-sized star.
Seasons are not caused by Earth's distance from the Sun; they are caused by the tilt of the axis.
Plants do not get their food from the soil; they make food from carbon dioxide and water using sunlight.
Most of a tree's mass comes from carbon dioxide in the air, not from the soil.
Plants get water and minerals from the soil, but not food.
Heavier objects do not fall faster than lighter objects when air resistance is small.
Cold is not a substance; cold objects simply have less thermal energy.
A blanket does not make heat; it slows the loss of body heat.
Rust is not paint; it is a new substance, iron oxide.
Clouds are not water vapor; they are tiny liquid droplets or ice crystals.
Bats are not birds; they are mammals.
Whales are not fish; they are mammals that breathe air.
Spiders are not insects; they are arachnids with eight legs.
Mushrooms are not plants; they are fungi.
Penguins are birds that cannot fly but swim well.
Viruses are not made of cells and cannot reproduce on their own.
Glass is not a good conductor of electricity.
Air is matter because it has mass and takes up space.
Light is not matter; it is a form of energy.

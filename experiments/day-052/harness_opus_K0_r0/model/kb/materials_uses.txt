# Materials, their properties, and why they are chosen for uses
Aluminum is used for soda cans and airplanes because it is lightweight, strong and does not rust.
Copper is used for electrical wires and pipes because it conducts electricity and heat well and does not rust easily.
Steel is used for bridges, buildings and cars because it is very strong.
Iron is used to make steel; iron rusts when exposed to water and oxygen.
Stainless steel contains chromium, which keeps it from rusting.
Gold is used in jewelry and electronics because it is shiny, does not tarnish and conducts electricity.
Silver is the best conductor of electricity but is expensive.
Tungsten is used for light bulb filaments because it has a very high melting point.
Mercury was used in thermometers because it is a liquid metal that expands evenly when heated.
Glass is used for windows because it is transparent and lets light through.
Glass is brittle and breaks easily.
Plastic is used for containers because it is light, waterproof, cheap and does not break easily.
Plastic and rubber are used to cover wires because they are electrical insulators.
Rubber is used for tires because it is flexible, strong, waterproof and grips the road.
Rubber balls bounce because rubber is elastic.
Wood is used to build houses and furniture because it is strong, renewable and easy to cut.
Wood floats because it is less dense than water.
Concrete and brick are used for buildings because they are strong and do not burn.
Clay can be shaped when wet and becomes hard when heated in a kiln to make pottery and bricks.
Cotton is used for clothing because it is soft and absorbs moisture.
Wool keeps people warm because it traps air.
Nylon and polyester are strong synthetic fibers made from oil.
Paper is made from wood pulp and can be recycled.
Cardboard is used for boxes because it is light, strong and cheap.
Sponges absorb water because they have many holes.
Paper towels absorb liquids.
Foam is used for packing because it is light and absorbs shock.
Styrofoam cups keep drinks hot because foam is a good insulator.
Ceramic tiles are used in kitchens because they resist heat and water.
Cast iron pans hold heat well.
Teflon coats nonstick pans because it has very low friction.
Leather is made from animal skin.
Silk is made by silkworms.
Diamond is used in cutting tools because it is the hardest material.
Graphite is used in pencils because it is soft and leaves marks.
Charcoal is made by heating wood with little air.
Salt is used to preserve food and melt ice.
Sand is used to make glass and concrete.
Limestone is used to make cement.
Marble is used for statues and buildings because it can be polished.
Granite is used for countertops and monuments because it is hard and durable.
Slate is used for roofs and chalkboards because it splits into flat sheets.
Gypsum is used to make plaster and drywall.
Talc is used to make baby powder because it is very soft.
Quartz is used in watches and electronics.
Helium is used in balloons and blimps because it is lighter than air and does not burn.
Hydrogen burns easily, so it is dangerous in balloons.
Oxygen tanks help people breathe and are used in welding.
Nitrogen gas is used to keep foods fresh in packages.
Carbon dioxide is used in fire extinguishers and to make soda fizzy.
Chlorine is used to kill germs in swimming pools and drinking water.
Fluoride in toothpaste helps prevent tooth decay.
Magnets are used in compasses, motors, speakers, refrigerators doors and credit cards.
Lenses are made of glass or clear plastic.
Mirrors are made of glass with a shiny metal coating on the back.
Solar panels are made of silicon.
Computer chips are made from silicon.
Batteries contain metals and chemicals such as lithium, zinc and acids.
Lithium batteries are used in phones and electric cars because they store a lot of energy and are light.
A material that is flexible bends without breaking.
A material that is rigid does not bend.
A material that is durable lasts a long time.
A material that is waterproof does not let water through.
A material that is absorbent soaks up liquid.
A material that is elastic returns to its shape after stretching.
A material that is brittle breaks or shatters easily.
A material that is malleable can be hammered into shapes, like gold and aluminum foil.
A material that is magnetic is attracted to magnets.
A material that is a good thermal insulator keeps heat in or out, like foam, wool and fiberglass.
An umbrella should be made of a waterproof material.
A raincoat is made of waterproof material such as plastic or rubber-coated cloth.
Oven mitts are made of thick insulating cloth to protect hands from heat.
Cooking pots are made of metal so heat moves quickly to the food.
A bridge must be made of strong materials that can hold heavy loads.
A boat must be made of materials that float or are shaped to float.
Life jackets are made of foam that floats.
Bike helmets are made of hard plastic and foam that absorb the force of a crash.
Football helmets and padding spread out and absorb the force of a hit.
Cushions and airbags increase the time of a collision and reduce the force.
Packing a fragile egg in soft material helps protect it when dropped.
The best material to keep an ice cube from melting is an insulator such as foam or cloth.
A metal container conducts heat, so ice melts faster in metal than in foam.
A black container absorbs more sunlight and gets warmer than a white container.
A shiny container reflects heat and light.
A clear plastic bottle lets light through; an aluminum can does not.
Engineers choose materials by comparing their properties, cost and environmental impact.
Recycled materials such as plastic bottles can be made into new products like fleece clothing.
